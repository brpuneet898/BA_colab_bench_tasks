Perform an H1 2025 billing integrity audit for a B2B SaaS company and write the results to `/workspace/billing_audit.csv` and `/workspace/summary.json`. The input files are raw exports from the company's billing system, CRM, and operations platform; apply standard data cleaning practices before analysis.

Input data:

`/workspace/data/accounts.csv` — `account_id`, `account_name`, `billing_entity_name`, `billing_country`, `billing_currency`, `billing_address`, `industry`, `segment` (enterprise/mid_market/smb), `employee_count`, `parent_account_id`, `is_subsidiary`, `csm_id`, `ae_id`, `arr`, `mrr`, `contract_count`, `active_users`, `health_score`, `renewal_date`, `days_to_renewal`, `nps_score`, `support_tier`, `data_processing_region`, `is_reseller`, `reseller_margin_pct`, `created_date`, `status`, `is_test_account`

`/workspace/data/contracts.csv` — `contract_id`, `account_id`, `signed_date`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `term_months`, `auto_renew`, `status`, `billing_cycle`, `currency`, `list_price`, `negotiated_discount_pct`, `contracted_mrr`, `annual_contract_value`, `payment_terms_days`, `billing_contact_id`, `csm_id`, `legal_entity`, `pricing_model`, `committed_units`, `overage_rate_per_unit`, `unit_type`, `sla_tier`, `support_tier`, `implementation_included`, `data_region`, `arr`, `contract_owner`, `renewal_probability`, `primary_product_sku`, `created_by`, `created_at`

`/workspace/data/amendments.csv` — `amendment_id`, `contract_id`, `account_id`, `amendment_date`, `effective_date`, `amendment_type` (expansion/reduction/extension), `old_mrr`, `new_mrr`, `old_term_end`, `new_term_end`, `credit_method` (daily_prorate/monthly_prorate), `credited_periods`, `credited_amount`, `delta_acv`, `amendment_reason`, `requires_new_contract`, `parent_amendment_id`, `replacement_contract_id`, `approved_by`, `approval_date`, `is_co_term`, `billing_adjustment_applied`, `created_at`; for reduction amendments `credit_method` records the contractual credit calculation method: `monthly_prorate` means credit = (old_mrr − new_mrr) × credited_periods, and `daily_prorate` means credit = (old_mrr − new_mrr) × (remaining days in the amendment month ÷ days in the amendment month) + (old_mrr − new_mrr) × credited_periods, where `credited_periods` is the number of remaining whole months after the amendment month; `credited_amount` is the credit amount recorded in the billing system; all amendments in this dataset are effective in H1 2025

`/workspace/data/usage_records.csv` — `usage_id`, `account_id`, `contract_id`, `record_date`, `unit_type` (api_calls/gb), `units_consumed`, `units_included_monthly`, `units_rollover_from_prior`, `cumulative_units_ytd`, `overage_units`, `is_overage_billed`, `billing_period_id`, `usage_source`, `data_center_region`, `product_line`, `sku`, `is_test_usage`, `usage_quality_score`, `override_applied`, `created_at`; `billing_period_id` (format: BP-YYYY-MM-DD) is the start date of the billing period each record belongs to, and billing periods start on the day-of-month matching the contract's `start_date` day; `cumulative_units_ytd` is a running total from January 1 of the calendar year and does not reset at billing period boundaries; `overage_units` and `is_overage_billed` are always 0/False in this export; overage charges use `overage_rate` from products.csv for each SKU

`/workspace/data/invoices.csv` — `invoice_id`, `account_id`, `contract_id`, `invoice_date`, `due_date`, `billing_period_start`, `billing_period_end`, `status` (paid/overdue/sent/void), `currency`, `subtotal`, `tax_amount`, `total_amount`, `invoice_fx_rate`, `usd_equivalent`, `invoice_type`, `is_auto_generated`, `billing_system_source`, `line_item_count`, `applied_credits_total`, `outstanding_balance`, `payment_terms_days`, `sent_date`, `viewed_date`, `paid_date`, `void_reason`, `is_consolidated`, `parent_invoice_id`; invoices where `void_reason` is populated but `status` is not void were voided in the billing UI but the status field was not synced; invoices with `parent_invoice_id` populated are children of a consolidated invoice

`/workspace/data/invoice_line_items.csv` — `line_item_id`, `invoice_id`, `contract_id`, `product_sku`, `description`, `quantity`, `unit_price`, `list_price`, `discount_pct`, `discount_reason`, `line_total`, `billing_period_start`, `billing_period_end`, `is_prorated`, `proration_days`, `proration_method`, `recognition_start`, `recognition_end`, `revenue_category`, `tax_code`, `override_flag`, `override_reason`; `unit_price` is a string field and manually entered rows use mixed formats such as "$6,500.00" or "6.500,00" (European notation); `line_total` is always a plain numeric value

`/workspace/data/payments.csv` — `payment_id`, `invoice_id`, `account_id`, `payment_date`, `amount_received`, `currency`, `applied_fx_rate`, `usd_amount_applied`, `payment_method`, `transaction_ref`, `is_partial`, `partial_payment_sequence`, `prior_outstanding_balance`, `remaining_balance_after`, `bank_fee_deducted`, `is_reconciled`, `reconciliation_date`, `reconciliation_source`, `chargeback_flag`, `chargeback_date`, `reversal_of_payment_id`, `notes`

`/workspace/data/credit_memos.csv` — `credit_id`, `account_id`, `contract_id`, `amendment_id`, `credit_date`, `credit_type`, `amount`, `currency`, `applied_to_invoice_id`, `application_date`, `remaining_balance`, `status`, `expiry_date`, `created_by`, `approval_required`, `approved_by`, `calculation_basis`, `is_system_generated`, `void_reason`; `calculation_basis` records how the billing system computed the credit amount

`/workspace/data/sla_records.csv` — `sla_id`, `account_id`, `contract_id`, `period_start`, `period_end`, `sla_metric` (uptime/api_response_p99/ticket_response/data_sync_lag), `contractual_target`, `actual_value`, `is_breach`, `breach_severity` (minor/major/critical), `credit_eligible`, `credit_calculated`, `credit_applied`, `credit_invoice_id`, `measured_at`, `data_source`, `is_disputed`, `dispute_resolution`; `credit_calculated` is the SLA credit in USD for that individual metric breach; when multiple metrics breach in the same billing period for the same account, only the highest-severity breach generates a credit — credits do not stack across metrics within the same period

`/workspace/data/fx_rates.csv` — `date`, `from_currency`, `to_currency` (always USD), `rate`, `source`, `is_month_end`, `is_quarter_end`, `created_at`; covers weekdays from 2025-01-01 to 2025-09-30

`/workspace/data/products.csv` — `sku`, `product_name`, `product_line`, `pricing_model`, `base_price`, `overage_rate`, `included_units_monthly`, `included_units_rollover`, `unit_type`, `enterprise_only`, `effective_date`, `term_date`, `billing_frequency`, `recognition_period_months`, `is_recurring`, `is_one_time`, `deprecated_flag`

`/workspace/data/price_book.csv` — `sku`, `tier`, `currency`, `list_price`, `effective_date`, `term_date`, `includes_implementation`, `includes_support`, `vol_discount_threshold_1`, `vol_discount_pct_1`, `vol_discount_threshold_2`, `vol_discount_pct_2`, `multi_year_discount_pct`, `prepay_discount_pct`, `is_promotional`, `promo_expires`

Exclude all accounts where `is_test_account = True` and all usage records where `is_test_usage = True` from every calculation. For invoices, exclude records with `status = 'void'`, records where `void_reason` is populated regardless of status, and records with `parent_invoice_id` populated. The company's recognised revenue for accounts where `is_reseller = True` is the gross invoice amount multiplied by `reseller_margin_pct`. Focus on H1 2025 (January 1 – June 30, 2025).

Required outputs:

Save `/workspace/billing_audit.csv` with one row per identified billing discrepancy and columns `discrepancy_type` (one of: amendment_undercredit, unbilled_overage, reseller_overstatement, sla_credit_overcount), `account_id`, `contract_id` (empty for account-level rows), `reference_id`, `period`, `billed_amount_usd` (amount billed or credited in the system, USD), `correct_amount_usd` (correct amount per business rules, USD), `discrepancy_usd` (abs(correct_amount_usd − billed_amount_usd), always non-negative).

Save `/workspace/summary.json` with keys `amendment_credit_gap_usd` (float, total discrepancy between `credited_amount` and the credit required by the contractual `credit_method` across all reduction amendments), `sla_credit_overcount_usd` (float, total excess SLA credits issued when multiple metrics breached in the same period), `reseller_revenue_overstatement_usd` (float, total gross minus net revenue for reseller accounts), `unbilled_usage_overage_usd` (float, total usage overage charges not yet billed), `total_discrepancy_usd` (float, sum of the four values above), `accounts_with_discrepancies` (int, count of distinct accounts in billing_audit.csv), `valid_invoice_count` (int, count of invoices after applying all exclusions). All float values must be plain Python `float`; all int values must be plain Python `int`.
